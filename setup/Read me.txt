Noorjahan for qBittorrent
=========================

Easiest: double-click "Install Noorjahan.cmd".
  - Installs the Inter font for your Windows user (no admin needed).
  - Copies all four themes to %APPDATA%\qBittorrent\themes:
    Light, Dark, and their Compact versions (tighter rows, 9 pt text).
  - If qBittorrent is closed, it offers to switch the theme on for you
    (your qBittorrent.ini is backed up first).

By hand:
  1. Copy one of the four .qbtheme files somewhere it can stay
     (Light, Dark, Light Compact or Dark Compact).
  2. qBittorrent > Tools > Options > Behavior: tick "Use custom UI theme" and choose the file.
  3. On the same page, set Style to windows11 (Windows 10: windowsvista). Restart qBittorrent.
  4. Optional: open fonts\Inter.ttf and click Install.

The theme works without Inter (it falls back to Segoe UI). Inter gives the exact look
and spacing it was designed with.

Made for qBittorrent 5.3 on Windows 10 and 11.
